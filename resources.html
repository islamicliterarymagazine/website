Resources

Resources

Places we turn to for writing help, research and looking at art, at Yale and beyond.

For writers & artists

  • IJMES Translation & Transliteration Guide

    The standard system for rendering Arabic, Persian and Turkish in Latin script. We follow it in every issue.

  • Poorvu Center for Teaching and Learning

    Free one-on-one writing support for Yale students.

  • Words Without Borders

    International literature in translation, including Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Turkish.

At Yale

  • Yale Chaplain’s Office — Muslim Life

    Prayer spaces, Friday prayer and the Muslim chaplaincy.

  • Yale University Library

    Including the Near East Collection of Arabic, Persian and Turkish books and periodicals.

  • Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library

    Islamic manuscripts and early printed books; the reading room is open to all researchers.

  • Yale University Art Gallery

    Free and open to the public. Search the collection for Islamic art and calligraphy.

Looking at calligraphy

  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Islamic Art

    A major collection with essays on calligraphy, ornament and the arts of the book.

  • Aga Khan Museum

    A Toronto museum of Islamic arts with a searchable online collection.
